==================================================
1. WHY C2 EXISTS
==================================================

The Prompt 035 Section-P live smoke exposed this behavior:

- During the onboarding interview, the owner volunteered a website URL
  naturally inside a free-text answer.
- Echo verbally acknowledged the website.
- The URL was NOT captured into brands.website_url.
- It was NOT captured into anchor_snapshot.website_url.
- It survived only inside the free-text interview answer.
- Therefore Sage did not receive that website as a structured discovery
  anchor.

Claude ruled that this does NOT invalidate the already-accepted Prompt 035
implementation because embedded/free-text URL extraction was outside the
previously accepted capture boundary.

However, it DOES invalidate restarting H1 right now because H1 is supposed
to measure the redesigned AFTER experience, and we already know a natural
owner behavior can cause the flagship website anchor to be missed.

Therefore one narrow corrective, 035-C2, must be designed, reviewed,
implemented, deployed, and live-verified before H1 restarts.

==================================================
2. BINDING C2 SCOPE
==================================================

C2 is CONVERSATIONAL CAPTURE ONLY.

Design the narrowest safe change that provides:

A. Deterministic URL candidate extraction

Detect literal URL candidates embedded inside free-text onboarding interview
answers.

This must be deterministic/non-AI extraction.

Do not infer a URL.
Do not paraphrase one.
Do not invent one.
Do not convert ordinary company text into a guessed domain.

Only a literal URL candidate actually supplied by the owner may enter this
flow.

B. Confirmation before structured capture

Detection alone MUST NOT create an owner-stated fact.

When an embedded URL candidate is detected, use the existing 023-style
confirmation pattern and ask the owner an explicit question such as:

"Is <url> your website?"

The design must preserve the distinction between:

DETECTED
and
OWNER-CONFIRMED.

Only owner confirmation may promote the candidate into the existing
structured owner-fact/anchor machinery.

C. YES path

After explicit owner confirmation, reuse the SAME accepted handoff used by
the existing whole-answer URL path:

- ownerEditFields / existing stated-fact machinery;
- existing provenance vocabulary;
- owner-provided/stated provenance under the already accepted mapping;
- anchor snapshot;
- onAnchorArrival;
- existing Tier-A research trigger;
- existing one-in-flight / budget / supersede behavior.

Do not create a second anchor pipeline.

D. NO / CORRECTION path

If the owner rejects the candidate:

- do not capture it as a stated fact;
- do not put it into the structured website anchor;
- do not trigger research from it.

If the owner supplies a corrected URL, that corrected literal URL follows
the same confirmation path before capture.

E. Honest acknowledgment

The Section-P smoke recorded Echo saying:

"thanks for sharing the website!"

when the website had not actually been structurally captured.

C2 must eliminate that misleading implication.

Echo must not narrate an embedded URL as successfully received/captured in
the structured system until the owner has confirmed it and the capture path
has succeeded.

The confirmation question itself should provide the honest acknowledgment,
for example:

"I noticed https://example.com in your answer. Is that your business
website?"

Exact wording may be designed in Stage 1, but the truth requirement is
binding.

F. Multiple URLs

Design bounded behavior when one free-text answer contains multiple literal
URL candidates.

At minimum:

- do not silently discard additional candidates;
- do not automatically classify all candidates as the business website;
- confirm candidates in a bounded, deterministic manner;
- prevent duplicate research triggers;
- preserve one-in-flight/budget controls.

Stage 1 must recommend the narrowest UX that satisfies these requirements.
