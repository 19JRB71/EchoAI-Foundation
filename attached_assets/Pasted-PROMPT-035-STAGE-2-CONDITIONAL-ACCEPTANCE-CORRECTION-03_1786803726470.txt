B. WHY THIS CORRECTION IS REQUIRED
============================================================

Acceptance review confirmed that H1-F2 is only PARTIALLY CLOSED.

The guided-wizard path now behaves correctly:

anchors arrive
    ->
Tier-A research begins within seconds
    ->
heavier consolidation can coalesce separately.

However, the primary conversational setup path still has a structural
delay.

Evidence from the live H1 experience indicates that the conversational
interview can run for approximately 25 minutes before consent/execution.

Under the current Stage-2 implementation, the brand row does not exist
until that late consent/execution point.

Because the existing research substrate attaches research to a brand,
Sage therefore cannot perform the new anchor-driven research during those
~25 minutes.

The result is:

owner supplies business identity
    ->
owner supplies more anchors
    ->
owner continues answering questions
    ->
Sage remains unable to use those anchors
    ->
brand finally exists
    ->
research begins.

That recreates the core H1-F2 problem on the conversational path.

It also weakens the intended Boz/Sage relationship:

Boz cannot ask increasingly intelligent gap-based questions during the
interview if Sage has not been allowed to investigate yet.

This is acceptance-blocking because the conversational interview is a
primary onboarding surface and Prompt 035's central product objective is
to make setup progressively productive rather than serial.
