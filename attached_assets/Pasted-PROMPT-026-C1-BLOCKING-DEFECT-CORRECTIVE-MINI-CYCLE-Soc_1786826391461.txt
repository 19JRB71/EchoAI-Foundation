C5. PAST-DATED ACTIVATION
------------------------------------------------------------

Do NOT silently activate stale posts.

Do NOT necessarily reject an entire calendar merely because one draft has
become stale.

Required intended behavior:

- only drafts whose scheduled_time is safely in the future may transition to
  scheduled;
- stale/past drafts remain unscheduled;
- every excluded stale draft is explicitly surfaced to the caller/owner;
- UI must not claim the entire calendar was scheduled if some items were
  excluded;
- owner receives accurate counts/status;
- valid future drafts can continue without forcing regeneration of the entire
  calendar.

The likely SQL guard is equivalent to:

scheduled_time > NOW()

BUT the predicate alone is insufficient.

There must also be reconciliation between eligible drafts and actually
activated drafts so stale exclusions are visible rather than silently left
behind.

Stage 1 must verify whether onboarding and regular content-calendar activation
share the same activation implementation.

If they do, correct the shared boundary once.

If they do not, STOP AND REPORT before duplicating logic across paths.
